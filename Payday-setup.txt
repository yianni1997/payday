PAYDAY - standalone version
No account, no sign-in. Your data is saved only in the browser on your phone.

PUT IT ONLINE (free, one time) - GitHub Pages
1. Make a free account at github.com (any email, not tied to Claude).
2. Tap "+" > New repository. Name it "payday", set it to Public, check "Add a README", Create.
3. In the repo tap "Add file" > "Upload files". Upload every file from this folder
   (index.html, sw.js, manifest.json, pdf.min.js, pdf.worker.min.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png). Commit.
4. Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder / (root). Save.
5. After a minute or two your app is at https://YOURNAME.github.io/payday/

The page code is public, but your money data is NOT. It never leaves your phone.

INSTALL ON ANDROID
Open the link in Chrome > three dots > "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app").

BRING IN YOUR HISTORY
Open the app > "Restore from backup" > choose payday-backup-2026-10-29.json.

BACK UP
Settings > Back up now. Save the file to Google Drive or email it to yourself.
Clearing Chrome's site data or uninstalling Chrome erases the app's data, so back up every couple of weeks.
The app reminds you after 14 days.
